ZoneIcons: modified subset of Material Design Icons by Pictogrammers.
Source: https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign-Webfont
Changes: 18 glyphs selected, remapped to U+E100–U+E111, fitted to 16×16 cells, renamed ZoneIcons.

Версия 2.0: каждая иконка разделена на два символа U+E200–U+E223, каждый шириной 8 px. Пара занимает две ячейки. Нужны одновременно новый шрифт и новые zones.json.

Version 2.1: icon artwork uniformly reduced from 14 to 12 px; two-cell advance unchanged.

Version 3.0 prototype: halves combined with outline union into one glyph per icon; U+E240–U+E251; 1024-unit advance, 12px artwork unchanged. Requires patched game width calculation.
